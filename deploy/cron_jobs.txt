Removed 2026-09-05 (deliberately, not accidentally): "L2 Publish Safety Net"
(enforce_publish_safety_net.py, every 5m) and "L2 Repair Incomplete
Completions" (repair_incomplete_completions.py, every 5m). Both were pure
subsets of what ticket_scout.py's reconcile() already performs synchronously
on its 2-minute tick (repair = normalize + unreviewable + ensure_missing_
reviewers; recover = orphan recovery -- reconcile() runs all of these plus
rejection/approval processing every 2 minutes). Running them as separate,
independently-scheduled cron jobs reintroduced process-level race risk
between concurrent OS processes touching the same Kanban tasks -- exactly
what Knowledge/L2_PIPELINE_STATE_MACHINE.md's "one reconciler owns lifecycle
sequencing" contract exists to prevent. L2 Ticket Scout is the sole
mutating cron backstop; L2 Kanban Completion Audit is read-only and safe
to keep independent.
